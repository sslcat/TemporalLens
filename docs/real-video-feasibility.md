# Can TemporalLens recognize events in real video?

**Decision, 2026-11-23.** I recommend a bounded local pilot on **IPN Hand**, using the original resized RGB frames and their matching original temporal annotations. The four events are **throw left, throw right, zoom in and zoom out**. Here “throw” means a hand command to a touchless interface. Success would establish gesture recognition on filmed people. Object manipulation and open-world recognition require other evidence. I have verified input access and a frozen encoder forward pass. Real-video accuracy, boundary quality and generalization are still open questions. [Source, licenses and access audit](real-video-access.md)

## Why these four events

A hand halfway through a sweep can occupy the same location moving either way. Zoom gestures add finger articulation and changes of hand shape that a marker cannot express. Pose, handedness and the hand's start or end position may still predict the label from one image. I want to measure how much those cues explain before attributing any gain to time. The corpus has four source labels of interest, G05/G06/G10/G11. Each occurs exactly once in each of the 200 recordings. Collapse all remaining annotated classes, including pointing, clicks and natural movement, into **other**. Keep the original IDs for error analysis. Other means outside the vocabulary. People can move throughout it. [Publisher vocabulary](https://gibranbenitez.github.io/IPN_Hand/) · [Original study](https://mm.cs.uec.ac.jp/e/pub/conf20/210110gibran_1.pdf)

I counted complete, consecutive target events after filtering to these four classes. An intervening other interval is allowed. A candidate pair fits into a short clip when its two annotated intervals span at most 450 original frames, nominally 15 seconds. These counts are feasibility checks on labels. No predictions or fitted parameters enter them.

## Smallest study I would run

1. **Establish the split before feature extraction.** Keep the authors' test pool held out. Use the released 148/52 source split as a starting point. The filenames also yield 50 four-take prefix pairs, split 37/13 with no prefix pair crossing sides. This is a useful cross-check. Metadata has background condition, lighting and people-in-scene fields, but lacks explicit person and physical-room IDs. There are only four distinct first filename tokens versus 28 published scenes. Obtain the publisher mapping or verify anonymous subject groups against the official split, and manually assign room/setup IDs from background contact sheets. Mark ambiguous identities or rooms unresolved. Do not infer relationships from face-recognition software. Freeze an auditable manifest of source ID, group evidence, physical room, take, shard, frame count, SHA-256 and split.
2. **Group before making any windows.** Connect recordings that share a person, room, source, take derivative or near-duplicate footage. Use connected components as indivisible split units. A room shared with retained official test recordings excludes linked development material from train/validation. Allocate remaining components by seeded, deterministic selection, with feasible gesture/order coverage and documented exclusions. Inspect hashes and perceptual frame matches across sources. No clips, windows, augmentations or alternate resolutions of the same source cross a split.
3. **Start with 56 recordings.** Target 16 train, four validation and eight test people, two recordings each, across at least four independent test components. This gives 32/8/16 examples per direction in the three partitions. Choose the training and validation sources without model scores. For natural short-pair evaluation, add held-out sources from the official 52 as needed, up to **92 recordings total**, targeting at least 20 eligible pairs across eight verified people and both orders for each inverse pair actually reported. Fix selection and test windows before fitting. Across the full official test pool there are two short right→left throws and zero left→right throws. A balanced short-throw-order claim is impossible here. Report supported pair strata and counts. For natural ordering across a full recording, all four targets are available.
4. **Prepare two views of the same recordings.** Use dense uninterrupted source streams for detection. Sample frame numbers 1, 5, 9, … (7.5 samples/s on the nominal released timeline). Convert an inclusive label interval `[a,b]` to zero-based `[a−1,b)`. Ignore unannotated frames and padded ticks in the loss and metrics. Index JPEGs by numeric filename, independent of TAR member order. Train in 90-tick chunks with overlapping context, retaining each center prediction once at evaluation. For UI-scale checks, derive contiguous 5–15 s excerpts. Include blind tiles and background-only negatives. For enriched pairs, center the minimal pair span inside a 450-frame crop, shift inside source limits, and record any additional target or truncation. Score only fully observable pairs, report censoring, and do not double-count overlapping excerpts as independent evidence.
5. **Check boundary meaning.** Two reviewers independently inspect at least 20 target intervals, balanced by gesture, plus ten other intervals from development. Record direction agreement, onset/offset differences, numbering gaps, mirroring, occlusion and missing-hand cases. Resolve mismatches and freeze exclusions before evaluating models. A sign reversal, unresolved room overlap or unexplained frame drift blocks the corresponding claim. If independent room groups or order counts are insufficient, report subject-only performance as such and reserve the missing endpoint for original capture.

## Models and controls

I would use frozen **DINOv2 ViT-S/14**. Letterbox each RGB frame to 224 × 224 using bicubic resizing and the encoder's mean color for padding. Apply its published channel normalization. Average the 16 × 16 patch-token grid into four spatial cells and concatenate the 384-dimensional vectors into a 1,536-dimensional observation per tick. This retains coarse hand position. Global pooling could obscure small fingers. Cache FP16 features once and share the exact cache, training sources, sampling, normalization, loss and decoder across learned heads. Pin the weight revision and preprocessing. The checkpoint is Apache-2.0. Record the provider, notices and any modifications for redistribution. Pretraining-set overlap with this footage has not been established. [Author model card](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2/blob/main/MODEL_CARD.md) · [Pinned checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/facebook/dinov2-small/tree/ed25f3a31f01632728cabb09d1542f84ab7b0056)

|---|---|---|
| Independent-frame baseline | Project 1,536→64, three residual pointwise MLP blocks (64→96→64), five logits per tick. Pool logits over known event windows for recognition. Also report one midpoint frame. | How much do appearance and pose reveal? |
| Order-invariant multiframe control | Mean embeddings over each centered 15-tick window, then the same pointwise head. At stream edges use the same replication padding as the CNN. | Does smoothing or seeing more poses explain the gain? |
| Temporal CNN | Same projection and output dimensions, three residual 64-channel 1D convolutions of width 3 with dilations 1, 2, 4 and ReLU. Fifteen ticks of ordered context, up to seven future ticks for offline inference. Head sizes within 1% of the frame model with the stated biases and no learned normalization. | Does local order improve the prediction? |
| Classical motion baseline | Farnebäck optical flow between successive released samples. Subtract median background flow from the image borders. Pool signed horizontal/vertical motion, orientation histograms and expansion/contraction about the moving region into a fixed spatial grid. Fit class-weighted multinomial logistic regression on training labels. | How far do translation and finger expansion carry the task without a neural encoder? |

Use a training-only standardizer where needed. For learned heads use class-weighted cross-entropy, AdamW, batch 16 × 90 ticks, up to 30 epochs, three fixed seeds, best validation segment F1@0.5. Give both neural architectures the same two learning rates and validation budget. Apply the same minimum-run rule and gap-merging procedure across methods, with the same small validation search. Report raw and processed predictions. Remove audio, identifiers and absolute recording time. Include a time-only empirical class prior as a shortcut diagnostic. The four prompted lists, alternating pose/action patterns and exactly-once classes make script memorization plausible. Original metadata marks 187 recordings as right-hand and 13 as left-hand. The paper describes students aged 18-25. Report the handedness counts per split and avoid conclusions about underrepresented users. The decoder must allow zero, repeated, missing and extra events.

## What would count as a temporal result?

- **Recognition.** On oracle target intervals, average the four target logits and report event accuracy, macro F1, per-class counts and confusion matrices. Also measure dense five-class macro F1 with other included. Score inverse-pair and midpoint ambiguities separately. Preregister a useful improvement of at least **0.05 macro F1** over both the independent and pooled controls on held-out components.
- **Localization.** Match predictions and references one-to-one by class and temporal IoU within each source, descending IoU with deterministic tie breaks. Report segment precision/recall/F1 at IoU 0.1, 0.25 and 0.5, onset/offset MAE in original frames for matched segments, miss rate, false alarms per nominal minute and median best IoU with zero for misses. Report a **0.05 F1@0.5** target over the strongest appearance control. The four-tick stride limits boundary resolution. Keep human boundary disagreement beside the model error.
- **Natural ordering.** Report exact ordered-list accuracy per full recording and per blind clip. Missing, repeated and extra targets invalidate the list. Also report unordered multiset accuracy, normalized edit distance and pair order conditional on both events being separately localized at IoU ≥0.5, alongside pair coverage and unconditional correct-pair rate. For pair accuracy, compare predicted start times to reference precedence. Count an unmatched endpoint as a failure in the unconditional rate. Macro-average AB and BA where both exist. Publish the actual denominators. A detector followed by chronological sorting implements ordered detection, with no learned long-range reasoning implied.
- **A task no static observation can solve in general.** For each pair of distinct held-out gesture bouts A and B, present the same two intact bouts in AB and BA order. Ask which of the two named gestures came first. Both examples contain precisely the same multiset of RGB frames. A bag-only predictor has a 50% ceiling on these balanced twins. Score a fixed first-bout versus second-bout readout of the class logits for all methods. Reset temporal context at bout joins and apply identical padding to both permutations to remove the edit-jump cue discovered in v1. This diagnostic supplies bout boundaries. It measures access to precedence combined with recognition. Natural uninterrupted recordings remain the test of joint detection and order.
- **Sensitivity and inference.** Independently shuffle frames within each oracle bout, realign per-tick predictions and score original labels. Re-run with reversed bouts and frozen weights. End poses survive both interventions, so report changes for each class and for both appearance controls. Treat reversed human gestures as counterfactuals. Assign an inverse label only when reviewers agree it depicts the opposite action. Require a preregistered 0.10 accuracy drop on the shuffled temporal model with invariant whole-bout frame pooling as a sanity check. Report a time-prior baseline, errors by scene/handedness and paired differences for all three seeds. Bootstrap test components 2,000 times for 95% paired intervals. Few independent rooms can make intervals unstable. Leave-one-room-out results and per-person scores should accompany them. Declare an advantage only if the measured gains cross their target margins and the paired difference intervals exclude zero. Assess the optical-flow baseline separately before claiming learning adds value.

## Local resource envelope

**One measured feasibility result.** I loaded the frozen checkpoint locally, using PyTorch 2.9.1+cu128, Transformers 4.57.1 and safetensors 0.6.2 in a separate library directory. The official 88,249,960-byte weights file matched the pinned Hub LFS SHA-256. Original frame IDs 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 and 13 were the first eight image members in archive order. Eight real 320 × 240 frames from training source `1CM42_21_R_#153` in frame shard 02 produced finite `[8,1536]` GPU features. Reversing the batch and undoing that permutation gave zero maximum absolute feature difference. With FP16, CUDA synchronization, four warmups and 25 timed passes, median/p95 batch time was **4.21/5.28 ms** on the local RTX 4070 SUPER (12,282 MiB). Peak live PyTorch allocation was **75.58 MiB** during the timed section. This microbenchmark repeats inputs already resident on the GPU. It excludes extraction, JPEG decoding, host transfer, process/context memory and head training. It measures throughput and compatibility only.

## Decision after the pilot

I would keep the product's real-video mode experimental until a frozen candidate beats the controls on scene-disjoint natural sequences, retains useful boundaries, and has documented failures on other gestures. A tie with flow still teaches which motion cues matter. A static or script-only win would argue for collecting more ambiguous examples before investing in a larger model.

If the subject/room mapping cannot be substantiated, or natural AB/BA coverage remains too thin, I would record **hands opening and closing the same hinged box** in a separate consented corpus. A practical design is 24 volunteers in 12 distinct rooms, two people per room, eight 5–15 s continuous takes per person (192 takes). Counterbalance genuine open→close and close→open by varying starting state, include one-event, idle and partial-action negatives, mix box instances across labels, and hold out people, rooms, source takes and selected box instances together. Have two annotators mark first sustained lid motion and completion in decoded timestamps, with disagreement recorded. Obtain participant releases covering intended commercial reuse, avoid bystanders/logos and publish original recordings only under an explicitly granted license. This is a collection proposal. No such footage or labels were created in this phase.
